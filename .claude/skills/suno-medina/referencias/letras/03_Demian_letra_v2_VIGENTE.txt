[Intro]
[fade in, clean arpeggiated guitar builds, warm pads open underneath, atmospheric cinematic build, no vocals, seamless transition to the first verse]

[Verse 1]
[young male countertenor, pure clean head voice delivery, high register, ethereal delivery, close mic'd, restrained, dry centered, guitar arpeggio stays sparse, seamless transition to the chorus]
Oh, Demian…
La vida se llena de espejos
y de humo en sus pasillos…

Y en ellos escucho
el eco de tus lamentos,
entre los barrotes de la jaula
que ata mi libertad y deseos…

[Chorus 1]
[young male countertenor, pure clean head voice delivery, high bright register, ethereal delivery, close mic'd, loosens restraint, soaring chorus end, indietronica hook, drums swell into full drive, luminous pads widen, hooky finger snaps enter, layered harmonies widen, seamless transition to the second verse]
Oh, Demian…
Los humanos claman
por el perdón del dios
que rige con inclemencia
y quien nos maldijo al nacer…

Como una deidad que anhela
vernos sumisos y rezando
como Abel, con miedo
a la advertencia de su castigo
que proclama la ley de su cielo…

[Verse 2]
[young male countertenor, pure clean head voice delivery, high register, ethereal delivery, close mic'd, restrained, dry centered, acoustic strumming, soft percussion, seamless transition to the pre-chorus]
Oh, Demian…
Sabes que tanto arriba,
como en el abismo…
No hay mandato,
ni libro sagrado
que doblegue
tu auténtico albedrío…

[Pre-Chorus]
[young male countertenor, pure clean head voice delivery, high register, ethereal delivery, close mic'd, restraint begins to lift, dry centered, layered harmonies widen, drums build with rising toms, luminous pads swell, seamless transition to the final chorus]
Oh, Demian…
Enséñanos
a no cargar más cadenas
en nuestros cuellos,
pues arrastran a confundir
la duda con la fe…

Una fe ilusoria
que nos llena de culpa
como la vieja historia
del pacto que se selló
en el jardín del Edén…

[Chorus 2]
[young male countertenor, pure clean head voice delivery, high bright register, ethereal delivery, close mic'd, loosens restraint, soaring chorus end, indietronica hook, drums drive hard, luminous pads widen, layered harmonies widen, seamless transition to the guitar solo]
Oh, Demian…
Naciste del mismo barro
con el que el dios artesano,
como tirano, desde su soledad,
forjó este mundo profano…

[Guitar Solo]
[no vocals, expressive slightly tube-overdriven electric guitar solo, gradual build, widening stereo, pads drop back, seamless transition to the bridge]

[Bridge]
[young male countertenor, pure clean head voice delivery, high register, ethereal delivery, close mic'd, restrained confession, dry centered, guitar counter-melody, restrained synth, melancholic lift, seamless transition to the breakdown]
Oh, Demian…
Aquel ser te teme
por tener lengua de serpiente
que susurra palabras
que desatan cadenas de plata
y guían a los demás a vivir
sin su falsa promesa de fe…

[Breakdown]
[young male countertenor, pure clean head voice delivery, high register, ethereal delivery, close mic'd, tender restrained, dry centered, guitar counter-melody deepens, restrained synth, anthemic slowdown, cinematic lift at the end, seamless transition to the outro]
Oh, Demian…
Solo tú hallaste la semilla divina…

Perdida en el tiempo,
pero que crecerá en un árbol
por tener raíces que bajarán
y sentirán la calidez del infierno…

Así como ramas que se alzarán
hasta llegar al ardor del firmamento
y sin miedo van a desgarrar
las ruedas que acogen el trono
del tirano que vigila en su quinto cielo…

[Outro]
[young male countertenor, pure clean head voice delivery, high bright register, ethereal delivery, close mic'd, fully loosened restraint, dry centered, firm low end, guitar arpeggios shimmer, seamless transition to the ending]
Oh, Demian…
Dime…
El que sabe…
¿Es libre o ciego?
(soft background oohs and aahs)
Oh, Demian…
Dime…
¿Sabes o eres ciego?
[soft decorating oohs]

[Ending]
[final chord rings out, intimate vocal tail resolves, mellotron chime fades last]

[Fade Out]
[polished gradual fade over the sustained final chord, delay dissolving, smooth even taper down to silence]
