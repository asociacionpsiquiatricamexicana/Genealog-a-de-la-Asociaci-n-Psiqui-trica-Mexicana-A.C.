# PHILÉMŌN — letra v5, VIGENTE
# Contenido del autor, pegado directamente con metatags ya aplicados.
# Distinto de la v4 (archivada en 04_Philemon_letra_v4_archivo.txt, no
# se borró). Transcrito verbatim, sin ninguna alteración de Claude Code.

[Intro]
[fade in, clean electric guitar arpeggio builds with subtle tape delay, warm analog pad opens underneath, grand piano enters, wide controlled stereo, no vocals, seamless transition to the first verse]

[Verse 1]
[young male countertenor, pure clean head voice delivery, high register, ethereal delivery, close mic'd intimacy, restrained and oneiric, dry centered, half-time feel, guitar arpeggio stays sparse, seamless transition to the chorus]
En un sueño olvidado
crucé umbrales
con alas de carmín…

Y me perdí
entre el abismo y la tierra,
y, de pronto, desperté…

Al abrir los párpados,
supe que debía revelar al mundo
lo que vi en aquel limbo…

Y, para encomienda tan alta,
tomé un nuevo nombre…

uno que no diré,
pues ya lo saben
quienes deben saberlo…

[Chorus 1]
[young male countertenor, pure clean head voice delivery, high bright register, ethereal delivery, loosens restraint, soaring chorus end, grand piano takes the lead, chiming electric guitars widen, drums swell into a fuller lift, dark saxophone answers the piano, layered harmonies widen, seamless transition to the second verse]
Así,
al piano de cola,
cada noche, en gala,
voy guiando el descenso
de los soñadores
con arias divinas,
hasta el fondo de su alma…

Y, sobre todo, a quienes,
con miedo y temblor,
se atreven a descifrar en silencio
quiénes son de verdad…

[Verse 2]
[young male countertenor, pure clean head voice delivery, high register, ethereal delivery, close mic'd, restrained, dry centered, brushed percussion, delicate acoustic strumming, seamless transition to the final chorus]
Y, al volver de aquel umbral,
comprenden
que en cada senda de este ciclo
puede nacer la luz…

Porque, aun donde acecha la perdición,
siempre habrá un sendero abierto
que los conduzca hacia lo divino…

[Chorus 2]
[young male countertenor, pure clean head voice delivery, high bright register, ethereal delivery, loosens restraint, soaring chorus end, grand piano returns brighter, chiming guitars shimmer, drums lift without hardening, layered harmonies widen, seamless transition to the bridge]
Y al piano de cola,
en cada noche
y al despuntar,
comprenden que,
aunque la vida
se enrede en mil laberintos,
siempre arderá una estrella
que los guíe
hacia el amanecer…

[Bridge]
[no vocals, clean electric guitar solo, slow build, widening stereo, detuned synth drone deepens, dark saxophone answers, sidechain pulse underneath, two-bar instrumental settle before the vocal returns, seamless transition to the third verse]

[Verse 3]
[young male countertenor, pure clean head voice delivery, high register, ethereal delivery, close mic'd, bittersweet reflective, dry centered, airy register, seamless transition to the interlude]
Y de la ruina
surgirá la llama,
capaz de abrazar
la vastedad del mundo…

Y su luz
no se extinguirá jamás…
[Interlude]
[young male countertenor, pure clean head voice delivery, high register, ethereal delivery, close mic'd, clean electric guitar counterline, restrained synth, dark saxophone accents, melancholic lift, dry centered, warm tape echo, seamless transition to the breakdown]

Ya no llevarán máscaras,
porque las cadenas
que creían arrastrar
nunca existieron…

[Breakdown]
[young male countertenor, pure clean head voice delivery, high register, ethereal delivery, close mic'd, dry centered, sub-heavy bass, muted acoustic guitars, subtle tape wobble, dark saxophone, melancholic tension, seamless transition to the outro]
Recuérdenme
como el espejo fiel
de aquello en que algún día se convertirán,
y de lo que alguna vez negaron ser…

[Outro]
[young male countertenor, pure clean head voice delivery, high bright register, ethereal delivery, fully loosened restraint, close mic'd, mournful tone, gentle tape delay, firm low end, seamless transition to the ending]
Ustedes son yo,
yo soy ustedes…

Ustedes son yo,
yo soy ustedes…
[soft main-melody oohs]

[Ending]
[final piano chord rings out, clean guitar arpeggio resolves, saxophone breath fades last]

[Fade Out]
[polished gradual fade over the sustained final chord, tape delay decays, smooth even taper down to silence]
