STYLE (campo Style de Suno)
════════════════════════════════════════
Ceremonial desert folk with modal drone, parched and visionary, bittersweet resolve with some hope.
Close-mic countertenor, pure clean head voice throughout, airy but clean, no chest voice, no fry, ethereal delivery, layered harmonies.
60 BPM, A minor, Spanish lyrics.
Sustained drone bed, tanpura-like shimmer, deep frame drum, distant ney, bowed strings in modal figures, distant duduk, sub-heavy bass.
Dry intimate mix, minimal reverb, close recording, hi-fi.
Sparse drone-and-frame-drum intro building from near-silence.
Hook-driven chorus with drone opening wide, ceremonial drums swelling, layered harmonies.
Ceremonial climax outro, drone and strings at their widest, long reverb tail decays into silence.
